FAQs

Common Questions, Straight Answers

Short answers to the questions that come up before almost every heating and cooling job.

How quickly can I get furnace repair in Hamilton, Ontario?

Most no-heat calls in Hamilton are seen the same day, and we treat a dead furnace during a winter cold snap as a priority. We answer the phone ourselves and send a technician from the Hamilton area, because losing heat in an older, draftier home is a safety concern, not just an inconvenience.

What types of AC repair services do you offer?

We repair central air conditioners and ductless mini-splits across Hamilton, handling refrigerant leaks, failed capacitors and contactors, frozen evaporator coils, and compressors that will not fire up. If a unit is on its last legs, we give you an honest replacement quote sized to your home.

How do I know if my heat pump needs repair or replacement?

Look for rooms heating or cooling unevenly, ice on the outdoor coil in mild weather, loud operation or short cycling, and rising bills with no change in use. Those typically point to a repair. Replacement usually makes more sense past about 12 to 15 years, with outdated refrigerant, or when a major part like the compressor dies, and we walk you through the costs of each option.

Why is duct cleaning important for my home?

Hamilton's older homes have ductwork that has collected decades of dust, pet hair, and renovation debris, and all of it restricts airflow and recirculates through your rooms. A professional cleaning opens the system back up so your furnace and AC run shorter cycles, cost less, and deliver cleaner air.

Can you repair or replace my thermostat?

Yes. We repair and replace programmable, smart, and zoning thermostats throughout Hamilton. If yours is misreading the room, short-cycling your furnace, or losing its Wi-Fi link, we get it working properly with your heating and cooling equipment again.

What types of fireplaces can you install?

We install gas, electric, and wood-burning fireplaces, with gas inserts being the most common request from Hamilton homeowners. Every unit is sized and vented to code and tied into your heating setup so it runs safely and efficiently.

Do you install ductless AC systems, and are they energy-efficient?

Yes. Ductless mini-splits are very efficient because they skip duct losses entirely and let you condition only the rooms you use, which is ideal for Hamilton home additions, upper-floor retrofits, and converted attics. We size each indoor head to its space and run the lines cleanly.

Are your technicians licensed and experienced?

Every technician we dispatch is fully licensed, insured, and experienced with the aging furnaces, boilers, and AC units common in Hamilton's older housing stock. You get a professional who shows up on time and stands behind the work.

Do you serve areas outside of Hamilton, Ontario?

Yes. We provide HVAC services throughout Hamilton and nearby communities including Ancaster, Stoney Creek, Dundas, Waterdown, and across Hamilton-Wentworth.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?

We do. A furnace outage in January or an AC failure during a humid Hamilton heat wave cannot wait for office hours. Call us any time, day or night, and we will get a technician out to you.
